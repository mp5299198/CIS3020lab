Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the Zelda universe.

Is Link always the same character?

No — each game usually features a new incarnation of Link.

What is the Triforce?

A sacred relic representing Power, Wisdom, and Courage.

Which game should new players start with?

Ocarina of Time, Breath of the Wild, or A Link to the Past.

Is there an official timeline?

Yes — published in the Hyrule Historia book.

Why does Zelda appear as Sheik?

To hide from Ganondorf and guide Link in secret.
